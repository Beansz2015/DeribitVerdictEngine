## 2. The substantive finding — D-A's anchor is the wrong quantity

**`AWARD%` is not the vote.** The packet's §1.5 and D-A justify their Principle A — *"`AWARD%` **is** the vote, whereas FLAT% is an intermediate"* — and target AWARD ≈ 50%. That premise is false, and I verified it in the code rather than inferring it.

`Core/ScoringEngine_Calculate_Scoring.vb:254` opens `Select Case r.SqueezeStatus`. The TTM award block at `:262–276` is nested inside **`Case "RELEASING", "NONE"`**. Under `Case "ACTIVE"` (`:255–260`) both sides take `cfg.Scoring.BbwSqueezePenalty` and TTM's state reaches only the note string — **it awards nothing regardless of its value**. With `squeeze_percentile: 0.20`, ACTIVE fires by construction.

Measured on the same 8,452 rows:

| ExecRes | n | FLAT% | AWARD% | **ACTIVE%** | **actual vote rate** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 6,648 | 0.69 | 64.23 | **29.17** | **43.37** |
| 3 | 1,804 | 0.61 | 63.91 | **24.50** | **46.29** |

**The vote already runs at 43.4% / 46.3% — at or below the ~50% the recommendation exists to reach.** Carrying the attenuation (43.37 / 64.23 = 0.675) onto the proposed ladder puts the true vote near **34–36%**, not 50%. So option (a) as specified would make TTM *less* engaged than the design point it is meant to restore, in the opposite direction from its stated intent.

Two further defects in the same ladder, independent of the above:

- **1.60 is not a measured ratio.** §1.5 and D-A describe the 3m value as carrying "its own measured ratio (1.60)". It is 40.0 ÷ 25.0 — the quotient of two independently-chosen round grid-points. The measured ratio is **1.45**. The packet's own argument against the ×2.1 proxy (that it over-scales) applies to 1.60 at reduced strength, ~10%. The ladder has no row between 30 and 40, so 40.0 is a grid artifact; ~37 sits nearer the target.
- **"Stable across anchors" is generous.** The ratio declines monotonically — p25 1.504 → p50 1.457 → p75 1.374 → p90 ~1.30 → p99 ~1.29, a ~15% drift. The load-bearing conclusion (nowhere near 2.1) survives at every anchor; the stability claim does not.

**What survives untouched:** `flat_threshold = 0.5` is ~100× too small in unit terms, the FLAT band does nothing, and the ×2.1 proxy over-scales. All three are arithmetic, all three reproduced twice, none depends on Principle A.

**Consequence.** D-A is direction-only per the seat-close handover §3, so nothing is blocked. But **whoever specs it must not inherit the AWARD% ladder.** The re-derivation needs to target the *post-`SqueezeStatus`-gate* vote rate, and to decide the 3m value from the measured 1.45 rather than a grid quotient. Recorded on the §12 TTM row so the next reader cannot miss it.

## 3. Rulings

### D-C · Does session-volume calibration park behind D3? — **RULED: yes, and the edge is recorded**

The mechanism is confirmed in code, not inferred: the threshold deliberately excludes the in-progress bar while the ratio's numerator *is* that bar, and `CalcVolumeSMA`'s denominator includes it too, compounding the same direction. Tuning a multiplier on a threshold the input does not reach is tuning a dial on nothing, and a closed-bar-derived multiplier would describe the D3 closed-bar arm rather than the live engine.

**With one correction to the packet's framing, which I am adopting rather than the packet's number.** D-C leans on the NY figure of **0.69%**, but the sessions whose multipliers are actually queued next (LONDON, NY per the v58 follow-on) include the ExecRes-3 populations, where §3.2's own "any-fire" rate is **2.66%** — roughly 4× higher. Still small enough that the ruling stands unchanged, but *"essentially nothing reaches it"* is a NY statement generalised, and the LONDON/NY passes should quote 2.66% rather than 0.69% so the argument is not overstated against them.

Sharpening the dependency, per the packet: `auto_run.trigger_mode` is **not a CSV column**, so the book cannot record which trigger mode scored a row — meaning a past or future change to it would shift the volume distribution invisibly. The instrument that would let you *verify* a forming-bar change after the fact does not exist either. It rides the next natural rotation alongside the J-E effective-source stamp; **neither forces one.**

**Also flagged, not a decision:** v58's *direction* (dial back an unjustified weekend-set notch) stands on its own terms, but its stated **mechanism** — the notch "suppressing trades" — can account for at most a fraction of a percentage point through the volume channel. The same reasoning is queued next for LONDON and NY and **should not be applied a second time** without this context.

### D-D · Close the two swing-pivot §12 knobs as "no change, evidenced"? — **RULED: yes, on placeability alone**

Both knobs are fine and the evidence is clean: widening the wing 3→5 costs **14.3pp** of placeability (45.5% → 31.2%), and lookback is not the constraint at the live wing — median pivot age **8 bars** against a lookback of 30, with lb45 moving placeability by **+0.1pp**.

**Two corrections to the packet's framing, and the first matters.** Handle #7 sells the trade as "2-for-1" — 7.5pp of *durability* bought for 14.3pp of placeability. The derivation's own §4 forbids that reading: the crossing column is **direction-blind**, and as §4 itself says, for a long position a crossed swing high is *the target being reached, which is a success*. §4 explicitly labels it "context only." The spec-back cell promotes it to "durability," which is precisely the substitution the derivation refused to make. **The recommendation survives — it rests on placeability, which is unambiguous — but the trade is not 2-for-1 and should not be cited that way.** Second: *"lookback is never the constraint, at any wing"* overstates it. True on placeability at every wing, but the *both-found* rate at w5 moves **92.8% → 98.9%** from lb30 to lb45. Lookback **is** binding at w5. Irrelevant at the live wing of 3, which is why the ruling is unaffected.

## 4. A §4 "cannot verify" item that turns out to be partly answerable

The packet §4 lists *"whether the repaired June is byte-identical to the original"* as unanswerable, since `backtest_data/` is gitignored and the originals are gone. **It is partly answerable, and the answer is no.**

The completeness sweep reproduces every published count exactly — except **15m zero-volume bars, which now read 19 (0.11%) against the published 25 (0.14%)**. The store only grew, so six 15m bars that were zero-volume before the damage now carry volume. **The repaired June is demonstrably not byte-identical to the original.**

**Nothing is contaminated** — no derivation in this batch consumes 15m, and the 1m/3m figures reproduce exactly. The reason it went unnoticed is narrow and worth keeping: the packet's §0 re-run table re-checked only the **1m** zero-volume figure. A completeness check that counts rows but samples only one resolution for *quality* can miss a resolution-scoped difference. Worth folding into D-F's standing convention: count all resolutions, and check quality on all of them too, not just the one the study consumes.
